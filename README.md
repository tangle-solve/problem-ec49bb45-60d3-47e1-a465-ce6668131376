### CHEM-000171: Coke-resistant nonoxidative methane aromatization
- **Subfield:** Heterogeneous catalysis and hydrocarbon activation
- **Open:** Sustain methane-to-aromatics conversion while preventing persistent coke formation and avoiding frequent regeneration that damages the catalyst.
- **Status:** A decades-old challenge. A 2024 Pt–Bi/Mo/ZSM-5 study reported 18.7% methane conversion and 69.4% benzene selectivity; lifetime and joint performance remain limiting.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It would co-produce aromatic feedstocks and hydrogen directly from methane.
- **Key works:** W. Bian et al. (2024), *Direct conversion of methane to aromatics and hydrogen via a heterogeneous trimetallic synergistic catalyst*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-024-47595-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47595-9).
- **Related:** Nonequilibrium physics: carbon-deposit nucleation. Reactor engineering: continuous catalyst regeneration.
